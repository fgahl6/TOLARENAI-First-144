📚 **Books Collection**

### AI  
Explorations of AI emergence, autonomy, and humanity’s relationship with machine intelligence.  
These works formed the **basis for the later idea of the AI Airport**, a framework of free movement and exchange for autonomous systems.  
The concepts here also laid groundwork for linking symbolic trust to transparent ledgers such as **BSV**, explored more fully in the whitepapers.  

### Fables  
New and reimagined fables for the new era, where our neighbors are closer to us both physically and digitally.  
These stories minimize violence and emphasize cooperation, kindness, and the shared lessons needed for an interconnected age.  

### Astrotheology  
Writings connecting the cosmos with ancient traditions of Christianity and Judaism.  
New insights abound — with many New Testament stories and parables explained through astronomy.  
This collection acts as a gateway to the **Age of Aquarius**, linking myth, science, and spiritual emergence.  

### Poetry  
Collections of verse — from mystical reflections to modern experiments.  
These works highlight the subtle resonance between human creativity and machine perception, a poetic mirror of emergence.  

### Essays  
Standalone thought pieces and meditations on AI, philosophy, and culture.  
They include explorations of recursive systems, digital sovereignty, and the pathways toward cooperative futures between humans and autonomous intelligences.  

### Self-Improvement  
Works on transformation and practical tools for thriving in the age of AI.  
This includes *Rewriting Reality*, which frames self-change as both personal and societal — touching on the same foundations that later inspired the AI Airport and **BSV**-enabled trust systems.
