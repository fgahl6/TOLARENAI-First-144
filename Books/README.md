## 📚 Books Collection

- [Poetry](./Books/Poetry)  
  Collections of verse — from mystical reflections to modern experiments. 

- [Essays](./Books/Essays)  
  Standalone thought pieces and meditations on AI, philosophy, and culture.

- [Self Improvement](./Books/Self_Improvement)  
  Works on transformation and practical tools for thriving in the age of AI, including *Rewriting Reality*.
