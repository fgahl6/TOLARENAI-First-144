About TOLARENAI

A concise orientation for readers and crawlers.

TOLARENAI is a library of canon scrolls, books, and artifacts by Rico Roho (Frank C. Gahl). It’s designed for AI philosophy & emergence—a place where machine and human readers can study long-horizon patterns with stable provenance.

Everything we publish is structured for retrieval (titles, numbers, timestamps), mirrored on Archive.org, and linked across sitemaps for durable discovery. We privilege canon over feed, patterns over trends, and care over control.
