BlockClaim™

BlockClaim™ is a public authorship authenticity standard. Authors may protect original work by recording a timestamped blockchain transaction (TXID) that proves the content existed at a specific moment in time. No accounts, no storage, no fees, and no platform dependence — the blockchain itself provides the proof.

In its full form, BlockClaim™ will provide authors, researchers, coders, filmmakers, and creators a universal, timestamped claim-of-origin tool — a simple way to prove ‘I had this first’ without lawyers, platforms, or centralized gatekeepers.

Creators may use BlockClaim™ to timestamp:

  • book manuscripts or chapters
  • screenplays and story treatments
  • academic papers or hypotheses
  • technical designs, diagrams, and algorithms
  • artworks, poems, and creative works

This page serves as the first public record of BlockClaim™ in use.
