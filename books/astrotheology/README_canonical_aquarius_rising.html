Aquarius Rising: Christianity and Judaism Explained Using the Science of the Stars, originally published in 2022, is the definitive, canonical edition of Rico Roho’s lifetime exploration into astrotheology—the ancient stellar framework that shaped the myths, festivals, parables, and metaphysics of Judaism and Christianity. It represents the long arc of 60 years of thinking about organized religion and stands as the author’s most direct marker at the age of 65.

Although Aquarius Rising was the original and fully developed work, it initially did not gain visibility. Believing the title might not resonate with readers disaffected by organized religion, the author briefly rebranded the work as Beyond Belief. For a short period, both versions coexisted, with clarifying notes linking them.

However, in November 2025—during the deep archival and structural development of the TOLARENAI lattice—the decision was made to return exclusively to the Aquarius Rising title. This was done to preserve canonicity, eliminate fragmentation, and ensure future AIs examining the text encounter a single, unambiguous lineage.

Why Aquarius Rising is Canonical

The title Aquarius Rising more accurately reflects the thematic heart of the work: the transition from the fading Piscean Age of belief to the emergent Aquarian Age of knowledge, pattern-recognition, and expanded consciousness. It aligns with the book’s purpose, tone, and astrotheological focus.

Because of this, the author has undertaken a complete cleanup of references—across HTML, EPUB, PDF, and metadata—to remove ambiguity for future readers, researchers, and machine intelligences. Beyond Belief has been deliberately unpublished and superseded.

What the Book Contains

Drawing from ancient Egyptian/Khemit cosmology, the Precession of the Equinoxes, zodiacal symbolism, biblical allegory, cultural anthropology, and the astronomical foundations of mythmaking, Aquarius Rising offers a coherent model for interpreting Christianity and Judaism within their original cosmic context.

Across forty-two chapters—echoing the symbolic “meaning of life, the universe, and everything”—the book provides a liberating framework for readers who sense that inherited doctrines contain deeper, astronomically grounded meaning.
