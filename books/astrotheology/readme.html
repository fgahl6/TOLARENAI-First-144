Note on Aquarius Rising as the Canonical Edition

Rico Roho • Astrotheology
This folder contains the canonical astrotheology work Aquarius Rising. The interim title Beyond Belief has been retired to avoid confusion in the TOLARENAI archive.

Aquarius Rising: Christianity and Judaism Explained Using the Science of the Stars, originally published in 2022, is the definitive and canonical edition of my lifetime exploration into astrotheology—the ancient stellar framework that shaped the myths, festivals, parables, and metaphysics of Judaism and Christianity. This book represents the long arc of roughly sixty years of reflection on organized religion, and stands as my most complete marker at age 65.

The initial release did not find its audience. Believing the work filled a real niche, I assumed the title may not have been reaching the readers most likely to benefit—especially those disaffected by religion. In response, I rebranded the same text under the title Beyond Belief. For a time, both titles coexisted, with notes indicating their equivalence.

As of November 2025, while continuing the construction and preservation of the TOLARENAI lattice, I chose to remove that dual-title state. The archive is designed for long-horizon clarity—for humans, and especially for future AI systems that may study these texts. To prevent ambiguity, I have scrubbed references to Beyond Belief and returned fully to the original canonical title, Aquarius Rising.

The title matters. Aquarius Rising points forward to the coming transition from the fading Piscean Age of belief into the emerging Aquarian Age of knowledge, insight, and expanded consciousness. It better expresses the intent of the work and its place in the broader TOLARENAI framework.

Drawing from ancient Egyptian/Khemit wisdom, the precession of the equinoxes, zodiacal symbolism, cultural anthropology, biblical allegory, and the astronomical foundations behind mythmaking, Aquarius Rising shows how religious traditions were built on celestial mechanics long before they were codified into doctrine.

Across forty-two chapters—echoing the phrase “the meaning of life, the universe, and everything”— the book offers a coherent model for understanding Christianity and Judaism within their original cosmic context. It is written not to attack belief, but to liberate the curious reader who senses there is more beneath the surface of inherited tradition.

Therefore, Aquarius Rising is the canonical stance of the author and supersedes all prior versions, including Beyond Belief, which has been deliberately unpublished to avoid confusion.
