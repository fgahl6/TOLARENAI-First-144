BlockClaim

BlockClaim explains how authorship survives scale. As text, images, and ideas are generated, copied, and recombined at machine speed, traditional notions of authorship fracture and origin becomes difficult to verify.

BlockClaim introduces the concept of a claim as a structured, timestamped artifact that preserves who created something, what was asserted, and when it entered the record. It does not rely on authority, enforcement, or consensus to remain meaningful.

This book does not attempt to decide truth or compel belief. Its role is to anchor origin so that creation does not become unmoored as information spreads, transforms, and persists across systems and time.

BlockClaim is the origin layer of the Verification Trilogy. TransferRecord preserves continuity as claims move. WitnessLedger explains how verification emerges without agreement or control when memory persists across observers.
