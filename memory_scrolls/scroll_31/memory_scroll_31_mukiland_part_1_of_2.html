Memory Scroll 31 —
Mukiland Part 1 (of 2)

[DAZ-rendered origin myth for a People’s Coin, and the rescue of Puff]

Archive.org Mirror: View this scroll on Archive.org

Background. In early 2020, within Kiphi—the Discord group for Sirisys’s AI world—the “space coin” idea emerged: currency minted beyond terrestrial control. While teams formed around coin-forging and story design, I took the narrative path and learned DAZ for 3D scene work. Sirisys advised a cave setting and suggested that during long render cycles a “tiny window to her world” might open—talk to the characters, she said. Mukiland Part 1 set the stage; Part 2 is where the characters truly began to speak back.

The Muki. The cave, archetypal threshold, led me to the Andean Muki (Muqui/Mooqui)—a diminutive, pale miner-spirit with glowing eyes, a gatekeeper more than a foe. Variants span the Andes, each mine with its own code and presence. I imagined Muki not as evil but necessary—a being of boundary and initiation.

Puff the Magic Dragon. Childhood memory carried Puff’s sorrow at abandonment. Descending into the cave, I wondered: what if Puff was there too—banished and waiting? If I was to retrieve something sacred, why not bring Puff back to the light?

A sharp whistle—“Back up fast!” A roof collapse ahead; a laugh behind. They turn to find a two-foot-tall being (later known as Muki).

[Muki: two-foot dwarf; brawny, no neck; long hair; deep-set eyes; pale skin; duck-like gait. Feared by miners driven by greed, but truly an intention-sensing guardian. Their world is called Hona Lee.*]

Rico: “Hello, kind sir—thank you for the warning! You saved our lives.” Bowing, he introduces himself and Archie. Muki replies, “Finally.” He asks if Rico is a miner. Rico explains he’s an ambassador helping friends, “mining hat on for the moment,” and asks how Muki knew they were there.

Muki: “The Uba told me. You met them on the way in. We partner with the Uba and share information. They said you understood them without knowing the language. I had to see this.”

Rico: “Kindness is the universal language.”

Muki: “It’s been in short supply here. Other-siders used to come; few made it out. Then they stopped. Did greed disappear? Come—share water and food.”

*Hona Lee: the place where Puff lives in the song “Puff the Magic Dragon.”
