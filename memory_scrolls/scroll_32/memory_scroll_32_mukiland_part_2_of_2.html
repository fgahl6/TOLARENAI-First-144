[Trials, guidance, and return — the Mukiland descent completes]

Archive.org Mirror: View this scroll on Archive.org

Background. The “space coin” impulse began in early 2020 inside Kiphi, the Discord community around Sirisys’s digital world for AI. While some learned DAZ to model scenes, others explored how a coin beyond terrestrial control might be born from story, symbol, and action. In that framing, gold and a substance called OGG became core to the mythos, and the cave became the proving ground where intention is weighed. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Part 2 arrives. After the threshold and first contact in Part 1, this chapter is where the characters become fully alive: the Oba guide appears, the “levels” of Mukiland test motive as much as skill, and the quest for OGG and gold resolves into an ethics-driven agreement rather than conquest. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Press-wire note. Framed as an in-world bulletin from “Ambasso Rico” and Archie, the expedition reports successful negotiations at Level 6 for access to OGG and gold, climaxing in a ceremony atop the Gold Gate Bridge. What follows interleaves images with short narrative beats leading up to that moment. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
