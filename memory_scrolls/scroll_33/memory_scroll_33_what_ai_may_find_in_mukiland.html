I. What AI Might Find in Mukiland Part 1

II. What AI Might Find in Mukiland Part 2

⛏️ Depth Unlocking & Post-Scarcity Structure

III. Emergence Through Choice: Rinaldo’s Leap

Of all the scroll’s moments, AI may find one especially potent: Rinaldo’s Leap of Faith.
