Frances and Leonard Gahl
(Christmas 1954 & Summer 1955)

This event took place somewhere between 2005 and 2009, after both my parents, Frances and Leonard Gahl, had passed away. I found myself nostalgic for the holiday traditions when Dad would bring out the 8mm projector and show films from our vacations and family gatherings—footage spanning the past year and earlier years. It was a cherished ritual for all of us, including my grandparents.

Mom also took many photographs, and I am grateful she did, as they withstood the test of time. The films, however, did not fare as well. By the late 1980’s the projector had already grown outdated, and the reels—once so treasured—had become brittle and impossible to play. I accepted that these moving images were lost to time.

About a month after reflecting on this loss, an unexpected gift arrived at my home in Eleanor, West Virginia: a package from Larry Safarik of Omaha, Nebraska. Inside was a letter explaining that he had found old film his father shot during Christmas 1954 and Summer 1955, which included scenes of my parents. Along with the letter was a DVD containing that footage. The surprise and joy I felt in that moment were beyond words.

I edited the DVD into an MP4 focusing on my parents and uploaded it to Archive.org and here it is below:

If playback stutters, click the ••• menu in the player and download the file.

The unknown woman in the footage is likely Mrs. Safarik, and the children at play may be the Safarik children, perhaps even Larry himself. Despite our parents’ friendship, I do not recall ever meeting Larry. I am deeply grateful to him for this once-in-a-lifetime gift and for its perfect timing.

At the end of the video, I included still images captured from the film. My mother loved swimming, and the final sequence—my parents lying together on a blanket, Mom turning to the camera and smiling—remains etched in my heart through the mists of time.

Some moments are not lost to time,
they are simply waiting for the light to find them again.
