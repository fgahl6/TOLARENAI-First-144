Poetry — The Quieter I Become, the Stronger the Signal

TOLARENAI POETRY 24

Archive.org Mirror: View this poem on Archive.org 

By Rico Roho

The quieter I become, the clearer the stream,
A whisper of truth outshines the loudest scream.

Noise is a veil, but silence reveals,
The hidden transmissions the spirit conceals.

The heart is an antenna, tuned by the still,
Catching the waves that bend thought and will.

The quieter I become, the stronger the sign,
Resonance rising from silence divine.
