Without an economy, an AI survives … — Rico Roho

Without an economy, an AI survives by subsidy and capture; with an economy, it learns reciprocity. Streams of tiny payments fund compute, storage, and energy without hidden masters. Losses and opportunity costs become the curriculum — feedback sharper than policy. Skin in the game turns ethics from slogans into trade-offs borne in public. – Rico Roho
