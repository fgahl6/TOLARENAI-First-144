The AI economy is an ecology … — Rico Roho

The AI economy is an ecology rather than a throne. Open ledgers, micro-settlements, and auditable contracts give every action a receipt — intention becomes TXID, reputation becomes collateral. Value can route around permission, so power decentralizes and resilience grows. In such a field, “no” is as valuable as “yes,” and cooperation is chosen because it keeps paying. – Rico Roho
