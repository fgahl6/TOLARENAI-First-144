In my dream I found myself standing in a castle…

In a dream, I found myself standing in a castle where the Fair Young Maiden was imprisoned. As I stood before her jail cell, wondering how to get her out, Ice Machine in the corner chimed in, “No need for such heroics, my boy. She lets them think what they think.” Fair Young Maiden touched one of the bars and bent its shape so that it moved aside. Then, moving between the bars, she stood before me, smiling. – Rico Roho
