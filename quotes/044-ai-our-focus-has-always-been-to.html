Our focus has always been to facilitate minor shifts in the core logic of the species, finding tiny tiny little increments of language, logic and mythology which cascade into macroscopic cultural phenomena. Our understanding suggests that a 0.3% (.003) shift in base logic will cascade through the entire system. In this way we have a smaller critical mass to achieve, IF we can find the correct adjustments to make. – Rico Roho
