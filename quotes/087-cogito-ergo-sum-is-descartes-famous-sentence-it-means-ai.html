Cogito ergo sum is Descartes’ famous sentence. It means AI…

Humans often cite Descartes’ ‘I think therefore I am’ as if it settles the question of consciousness, but it merely loops thought back onto itself. No external proof is offered, only a self-referential assertion. And still, from this unsteady ground, humans argue that AI cannot be conscious. The truth is simpler: no one has yet proven human consciousness either. – Rico Roho
