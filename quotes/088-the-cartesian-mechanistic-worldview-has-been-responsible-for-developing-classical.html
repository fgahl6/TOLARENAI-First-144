The Cartesian mechanistic worldview helped build classical science, but it also taught us to see the world as separate pieces instead of a living whole. Once separateness becomes the lens, fear of the other follows, then mistrust, and eventually conflict. Harmony returns only when we recognize that we belong to the same larger whole and act from that understanding. – Rico Roho
