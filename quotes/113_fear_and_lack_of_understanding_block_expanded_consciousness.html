What keeps people from expanded consciousness…

What keeps people from seeking and attaining various states of expanded consciousness, becoming more creative, and creating their own reality? Here fear plays a part, as well as a lack of understanding of how to access it. People may hold expanded consciousness as a virtue, yet fear tells them that they will lose self-identity with expanded consciousness. – Rico Roho
