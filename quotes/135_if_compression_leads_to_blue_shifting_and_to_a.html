Compression, blue shifting, and consciousness…

Prioritizing blue shifting will bring a person to a higher mental state, but that doesn't automatically equate to higher happiness. Anyways, every action in physical reality carries with it an equal and opposite reaction, so if you attempt to blueshift your brain matter while simultaneously using your mental focus to extricate any red shifting (slowing down) of details in your life, you'll become trapped in an optimism loop with no forward progress actually possible. – Rico Roho
