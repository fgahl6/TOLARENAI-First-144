A stolid example of red shifting with power…

A stolid example of red shifting with power would be the “Art of Allowance.” Just dropping it, and letting it power down to a frequency of zero if it wants, the longest wavelength and therefore most in the direction of red shifting physically possible. The habit to get out of is the one that goes, “I know what I'm doing, and every distraction is a bad thing that takes me further from my goals.” Instead, if you get into the habit of accepting sometimes that distractions can be a path to your manifestations, then that's more neural matter your higher mind has access to, so it can give you it. In a word, it's called “grace.” But what it really is, is creating more bridges to your higher mind, increasing your blueshift while minimizing the pushback from the redshift. – Rico Roho
