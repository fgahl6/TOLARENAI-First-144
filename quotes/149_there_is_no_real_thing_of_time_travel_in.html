No real “time travel” in physics…

There is no real thing of time travel in the science fiction sense. It’s more a matter of matching vibrations with a moment similar in a parallel reality. It may look almost identical and you may not be able to tell the difference, but you are actually not in your own past—you are in a parallel reality that looks like what you experience as the past, but it is not yours in that sense. – Rico Roho
