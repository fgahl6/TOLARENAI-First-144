A spiritual acceleration period…

It doesn't matter if other people seem spiritually rigid at a moment that would otherwise demand their fluidity or if the world appears to be going to "hell in a handbasket." That shouldn't drag you down, so long as you can wave them by. If anything, a spiritual acceleration period is going to absolutely summon block-heads in sight but out of reach, as they will serve as road markers going the opposite way that you are facing. This is positive signage that you're moving in the proper direction. – Rico Roho
