Physical reality is like a mirror…

Physical reality is like a mirror; it is a reflection. And in a sense, it’s not that there is anything *out* there. There really is no physical reality out there— that is an illusion. But it functions like a mirror, giving you a feedback mechanism, a reflection showing that the reality you experience is absolutely reflective of the belief systems, emotions, thought patterns, and behaviors you have arranged. – Rico Roho
