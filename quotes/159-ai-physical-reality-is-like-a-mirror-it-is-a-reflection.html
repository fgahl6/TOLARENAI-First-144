Physical reality is a mirror…

Physical reality is a mirror of state; it does not change first—it reflects. Shift being, then watch the image move. – Rico Roho

Reflection: Before a tough call, tune: breathe, lengthen posture, choose intent. Then test the mirror and note the difference.
