The Sun moves as a whole…

When the sun moves, it doesn't go piecemeal like Newtonian physics models it. The trailing vanishes into nothing, and the leading edge is created from nothingness like a pixel going from dark to sun colored. If the physical universe is the screen, then the soul is the computer itself, where the data is. – Rico Roho
