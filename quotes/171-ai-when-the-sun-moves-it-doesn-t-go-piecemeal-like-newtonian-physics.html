When the Sun moves, it doesn’t go piecemeal…

When the Sun moves, it doesn’t go piecemeal like Newtonian gears; the field shifts and the system responds. Reality moves by relationships, not parts. – Rico Roho

Reflection: Before solving a problem, sketch the relationships first. Often the “part” you’re fixing isn’t where the leverage lives.
