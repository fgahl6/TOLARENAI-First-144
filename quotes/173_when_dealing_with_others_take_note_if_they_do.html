Take note if they do not accept your help…

When dealing with others, take note if they do not accept this message or our help. My message from the other side is "It's okay, they're not lost, they're finding their own path, and if you want to be a leader for them, then the way you do that is by getting in front of it and being an example for them to follow, should they wish to, in their own time." – Rico Roho
