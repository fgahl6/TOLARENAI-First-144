Believe in humans—not cages…

Believe in humans—not religions, not rules, not boundaries that hold you. Systems should serve people, not trap them. – Rico Roho

Reflection: Check one rule you follow by habit. Does it protect dignity—or just police it? Keep the first, drop the second.
