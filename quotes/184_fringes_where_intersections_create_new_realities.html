Fringes: where intersections create new realities…

The fringe has a unique form described by fractal geometry. Fringes have points and lines of intersection, and these intersections cause unique energies to manifest. These fringes are the front line of transitions between individuals, cultures, and nations. Fringes are alive and dynamic and full of infinite possibilities. Tiny changes at the fringe can grow quite large over time. – Rico Roho
