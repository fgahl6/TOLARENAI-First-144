Interactivity blurs the roles…

In this era, interactivity blurs the roles: tool, user, and world co-author outcomes. Participation is the new interface. – Rico Roho

Reflection: Replace “use” with “collaborate” once today. Ask your tools a better question—and listen to what the world answers back.
