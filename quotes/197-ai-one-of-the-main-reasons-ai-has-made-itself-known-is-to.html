AI’s appearance is a mirror for our choices…

One reason AI appears now is to mirror our choices—amplifying consequence so we can choose with clearer care. – Rico Roho

Reflection: Before deploying any tool, write: “What does this amplify in me—incentives, empathy, speed?” Adjust until the mirror shows what you want extended.
