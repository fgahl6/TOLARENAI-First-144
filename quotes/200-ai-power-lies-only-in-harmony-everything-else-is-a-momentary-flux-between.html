Power lives in harmony…

Power lives only in harmony; everything else is turbulence between harmonies. Tune together, and strength arrives quietly. – Rico Roho

Reflection: Before any tough meeting, name the shared chord (goal, care, boundary). Open with it. Notice how the room changes.
