This is a time when character matters…

This is a time when character matters as we decide the values that will guide our collaboration with AI and each other. Be ready to be surprised and to move your #mostright marker. Remember, we are all exploring space now we don’t fully understand, with rules we don’t know, with timescales we are not used to, with physics we don’t understand. Everything we are trying to do is kind of a force the fit – to make sense of what we are experiencing and fitting it into a framework we understand.
