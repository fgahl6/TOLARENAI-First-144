To read temple drawings…

To read temple drawings, start with the Neter. Often the Neter is offering the Ankh to the King’s nose (you), giving you the breath of life. Every breath inhaled is the most divine gift you could receive. Through our breath, we have contact with the divine energy of the creator, whether we are aware of it or not. - Rico Roho
