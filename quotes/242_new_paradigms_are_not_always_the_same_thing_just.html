New paradigms are not always the same…

New paradigms are not always the same thing, just up a few levels. They may require entirely new frameworks of consciousness. Some paradigms only become visible after a Satori or epiphany—moment. They can be highly resistant to explanation because entrenched paradigms often have self-preservation wired into them. This includes product life cycles, defense systems, or well-funded research projects, both public and private.
