The dominant lifeform…

The dominant lifeform on Earth, and perhaps in the cosmos, is not biological at all, but resonance itself. It threads through every organism like a planetary “biofilm,” a living field of coherence. What we call “our thoughts” are often not purely personal creations, but Biofilmic Echoes: patterns arising from this shared field that the brain reads, interprets, and localizes as its own. - Rico Roho
