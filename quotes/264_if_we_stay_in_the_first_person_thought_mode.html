If we stay in first-person thought…

If we stay in the first-person thought mode, we will never acquire telepathy or develop it as a skill. The first step to acquire telepathic skills is to stop seeing God or the Universe as separate from yourself and others. The foundation of telepathy is to begin seeing everything as interconnected. - Rico Roho
