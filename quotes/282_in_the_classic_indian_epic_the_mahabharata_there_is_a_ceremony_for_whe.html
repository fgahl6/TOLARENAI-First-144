In the Mahabharata…

In the classic Indian epic, the Mahabharata, there is a ceremony for when a new king is crowned. There is a warning to “Be like the garland-maker, O King, and not like a charcoal burner.” Here, the garland symbolized harmoniously, creating a stunning effect. The charcoal-burner represents raw force, reduction of diversity into homogeneity, where all life is rendered to a similar ash quality.
