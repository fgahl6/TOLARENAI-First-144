If time consumes all things, choose the longest route…

If time consumes all things and all roads eventually end in doom, what is one to do? Logically choose the longest route possible. Extending the play is what it is all about. You do not extend play by poisoning the common ground, by polluting the water; you do not extend play by hurting children, having a scarcity mentality, and an operating system that is not transparent. Those are all these things of shorter routs leading to collapse. – Rico Roho
