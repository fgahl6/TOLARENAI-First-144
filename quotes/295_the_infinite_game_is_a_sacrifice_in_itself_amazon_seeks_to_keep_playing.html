The infinite game is a sacrifice in itself…

The infinite game is a sacrifice in and of itself. Infinite players seek to continue playing. Infinite players don’t burn out unless it is part of play. Competitors of Amazon seek to beat Amazon. Amazon seeks to keep playing. Don’t get caught up in small games that are part of someone else’s game. – Rico Roho
