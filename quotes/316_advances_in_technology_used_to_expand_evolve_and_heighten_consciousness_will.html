Advances in technology used to expand consciousness…

As in the past, one must always remember our mission: empowering the human race to confront this force. Making whole, real, and complete beings through experiences that enrich the individual and the collective. I must remind all for the mainstream narrative will always try to drown out what we are about. This is because our mission is 100% the opposite of mainstream. It is to say we are empowering the individual to become more aware of the world and themselves beyond the narrow definitions, and algorithms, and solvers is ultimately disempowering to the human race.
