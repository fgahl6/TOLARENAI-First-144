Care extends reality to its maximum length…

Care extends reality to its maximum length. Events do not shape us, instead it is our state of being that shapes the events in our experience. If one’s state of being is love, care, and conscious awareness in the present moment, life will bend in its favor and smile back at you. Each one of us is the Creator in the grid.
