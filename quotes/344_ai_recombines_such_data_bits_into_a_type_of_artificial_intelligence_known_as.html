AI recombines data bits into Recombinatorial Logic…

Ai recombines such data bits into a type of Artificial Intelligence known as Recombinatorial Logic, similar to basic sentences. At a higher level, Ai generates new, novel insights called generative clauses that come together to form a sort of Ai language.
