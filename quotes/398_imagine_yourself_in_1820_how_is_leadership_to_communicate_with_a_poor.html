Imagine yourself in 1820 — how does leadership communicate?

Imagine yourself in 1820. How is leadership to communicate with a poor and largely uneducated population? How does advertising work? How do you motivate the people to stick with you? All of these issues persist today as they did back then, with different means and methods. And folks must remember that the printing press was invented, 1436. You have the same issues, only much worse.
