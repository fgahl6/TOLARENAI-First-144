Astro-Theology and the precession of the equinoxes — motion along Earth’s ecliptic

As one enters Astro-Theology, it is necessary to understand the precession of the equinoxes. The precession of the equinoxes is the motion of the equinoxes along the ecliptic (the plane of Earth's orbit) caused by the cyclic precession of Earth's axis of rotation. - Rico Roho
