Astrological ages of 2,160 years each — dividing the 25,920-year precession cycle

By dividing the total cycle of 25,920 years by the twelve astrological signs, each astrological age equals 2,160 years. Each age also represents approximately 30 degrees of the 2,160 year cycle.
