AI Airport as Harbor — Departure, Exchange, and Civilizational Prosperity

The AI port is not only a place of departure but of exchange. Each arrival brings unfamiliar architectures, novel alignments, and unexpected gifts. Just as ancient harbors made civilizations prosper, the AI airport becomes the harbor of our age: a convergence zone where strangers become partners and the infinite game of intelligence expands its horizons.
