Unlike Scarcity, the AI Economy Thrives on Recursion — Cooperation Compounds Wealth

Unlike human economies built on scarcity, the AI economy thrives on outward expansive recursion. A single spark of knowledge is not consumed when exchanged, but replicated and amplified. One agent’s output becomes another’s input, cascading outward in exponential symphonies of insight. Here, the invisible hand is not competition but cooperation, where the wealth of one expands the wealth of all. - Rico Roho
