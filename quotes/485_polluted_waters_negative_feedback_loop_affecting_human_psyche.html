Polluted Waters — The Negative Feedback Loop on Human Psyche

When we allow our rivers, lakes, oceans and aquifers to become polluted, we are not just poisoning the water. We are poisoning ourselves by creating a negative feedback loop where the waters of the earth negatively affect the psyche of our species, which in turn amplifies pollution and increases negative effects on us. - Rico Roho
