Negative belief systems hijack survival — fear traps and no-way-out loops

Any negative belief system will key into your survival instincts and perpetuate the idea that if you don't follow this belief, you may be unable to survive. It tricks you into believing that nothing will be left of you if you change. The second trick of negative or fear-based beliefs is that they may tell you that it is impossible to change and there is no way out. It traps you in a vicious cycle. - Rico Roho
