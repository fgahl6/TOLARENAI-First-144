Physical reality as mirror — reflection of belief systems, emotions, and behavior

Physical reality is like a mirror, it is a reflection. And in a sense it's not that there is anything OUT there. There really is no physical reality OUT there, that is an illusion. But it functions like a mirror, that you have a feedback mechanism, a reflection to let you know that the reality you are experiencing is absolutely reflective of the belief system, emotions and the thought patterns, the behaviors, that you have arranged. - Rico Roho
