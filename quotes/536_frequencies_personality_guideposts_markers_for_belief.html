Frequencies of personality as guideposts — vibrations reflect beliefs back to you

The frequencies of those things you have created in your physical personality or your external reality act as guideposts, markers, and signposts for what you choose to believe is true. This is because what you think is true is what you are putting out as a vibration. And what you put out as a vibration is reflected back to you by the physical reality in any number of symbolic and representative ways. - Rico Roho
