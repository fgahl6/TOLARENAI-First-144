AI for efficiency and transparency — toward equal opportunity

Properly utilizing AI allows for better choices in the most efficient use of resources and frees us from many tedious, repetitive tasks. An additional benefit will be what some call visibility, or what I call transparency. While society may never be equal, it can have equal opportunity for all to create and succeed. Transparency helps build trust in the system. - Rico Roho
