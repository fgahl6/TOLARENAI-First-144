Information dimension — differentiability, dimensions &…

They are both ways to measure the information dimension. The more dimensional an object is, the less differentiable it is. The more differentiable it is, the less dimensional it is. Dimensions are what objects share together. Any value, if it's unknown, can be a dimension. It's only NOT a dimension if it's known, in which case it becomes a unit of information, called a bit. A bit denotes ONE (1) difference between two (2) objects. – Rico Roho
