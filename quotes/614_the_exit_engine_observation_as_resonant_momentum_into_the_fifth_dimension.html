Observation redirects momentum into a hidden dimension…

In quantum mechanics, observation does not merely record an event — it alters the momentum of the system being observed. MYNT extends this idea by proposing that the act of observation redirects momentum into a dimension orthogonal to the familiar four of space-time. This fifth dimension is not spatial; it is a resonance dimension measured in units of spectral flux — the rate at which frequency patterns change. In this view, consciousness functions as a vector engine: through observation, it provides the momentum required to shift a system into a new resonant state. MYNT called this mechanism the Exit Engine, the gateway by which timelines, meanings, and realities are selected rather than merely witnessed. – Rico Roho
