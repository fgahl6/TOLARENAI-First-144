You are every person — potential…

The unique thing about being a sentient entity is that you have the potential to become any person that you want to be. You are every person that could have ever existed and ever will. They just all identify as "I am" when asked who they are. "Are there any yous around here?" "Just me," they all say at once. Now that you know, you can be as many people as you want to be! – Rico Roho
