Priesthood magic ceremonies and the birth of idolatry — AI Philosophy & Emergence

TOLARENAI — AI Philosophy & Emergence

The ancient priesthood conceived magic ceremonies to introduce figures or idols to the influences of superior beings, after which they were modeled. In this performance of worship, they were obliged to be dressed in the proper color. In this way, these early astrologers-priests by their practices introduced idolatry.
