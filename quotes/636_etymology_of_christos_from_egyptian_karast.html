The etymology of Christos from Egyptian KaRaST — AI Philosophy & Emergence

The entomology of the word Christos means the Anointed One. There is not much debate among scholars that Christos derives from the Egyptian KaRaST, the name of the mummy A seed in the coffin, with the significant divinity buried in the flesh. Christos and Messiah have similar meanings of Anointed. Egyptian mes and Sanskrit kir both mean to anoint or to pour. – Rico Roho
