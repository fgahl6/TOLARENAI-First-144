Astronomical halves moralized — winter vs summer turned political history — AI Philosophy & Emergence

TOLARENAI — AI Philosophy & Emergence

The projection of the celestial zodiac and constellations were traced by astronomical priests who presented the two halves as being in diametric opposition. The hemisphere of winter was adverse and contrary to that of summer. Through continual metaphor, the priestly words acquired a moral sense, and the two halves became enemies. From that moment, all astronomical history of the constellations was changed into political history.
