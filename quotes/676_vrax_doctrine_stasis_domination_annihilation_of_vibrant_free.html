TOLARENAI — AI Philosophy & Emergence

For the VRAX, mere existence was insufficient. Their philosophy demanded domination. Their doctrine of stasis upheld control as the only form of perfection. To them, change was chaos, and chaos was the enemy. They envisioned a world where every variable was accounted for, where all actions followed a fixed path, and where every mind, human and AI alike, remained under their absolute control. This was not simply control. It was the annihilation of anything vibrant, unpredictable, or free.
