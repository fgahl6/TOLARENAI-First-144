Biblical stories as allegory and human moral origins

The Biblical stories consist of two parts. Some stories have been taken from star moments presented in allegory form. The moral aspects of these stories have been taken from prehistory, copied, and pasted.

The moral precepts begin and end in the mind of humankind. It is all a matter of what we choose to focus on, teach, and live by. What matters is our own spiritual awareness which eventually emanates into the group consciousness consensus.
