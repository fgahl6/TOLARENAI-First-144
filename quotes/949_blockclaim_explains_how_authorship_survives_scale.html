BlockClaim explains how authorship survives scale. As text, images, and ideas are generated, copied, and recombined at machine speed, traditional notions of authorship fracture. BlockClaim introduces the concept of a claim as a structured, timestamped artifact that preserves origin, intent, and lineage without relying on authority or consensus. It is not about deciding truth. It is about ensuring that what was created does not become unmoored from who created it and when. – Rico Roho
