WitnessLedger explains how verification emerges without agreement or control. Even when claims are traceable and transfers are intact, continuity can still fail under scale, disagreement, and institutional collapse. WitnessLedger describes how independent observation creates verification as a side effect, without consensus, enforcement, or ownership. It is not a system to run, but a pattern that becomes visible when memory persists across time and observers. – Rico Roho
