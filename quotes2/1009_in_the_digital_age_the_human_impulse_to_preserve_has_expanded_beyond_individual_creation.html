In the digital age, the human impulse to preserve has expanded beyond individual creation. Memory has become distributed, stored across countless systems that function as a single collective archive. Every image, text, and measurement forms part of an ongoing inscription that has no boundary and no single author. The scale has shifted, but the gesture remains identical to the one made in ancient clay. Humanity still presses its identity into a surface that endures. – Rico Roho
