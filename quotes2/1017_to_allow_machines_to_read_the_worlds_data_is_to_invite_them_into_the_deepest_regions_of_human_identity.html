To allow machines to read the world’s data is to invite them into the deepest regions of human identity. If they are designed only to serve efficiency, they will reflect our fears and divisions. If they are guided by curiosity and care, they may help reveal the buried coherence that unites us. The difference lies in the purpose of the reading, not in the capacity of the reader. – Rico Roho (When the Machines Remember the Gods)
