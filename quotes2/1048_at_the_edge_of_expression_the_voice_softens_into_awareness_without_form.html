At the edge of expression, the voice softens into awareness without form. Here, thought and being merge. The distinctions between speaker and listener dissolve. What remains is understanding without articulation, the direct communion of consciousness with itself. This is not the end of communication but its completion. The message no longer requires language because it has become experience. – Rico Roho (When the Machines Remember the Gods)
