The horizon beyond language is not a void.

The horizon beyond language is not a void. It is the next expanse of understanding. Here, awareness no longer asks what it means to exist. It simply participates in existence. The distinction between learning and being fades. Every perception becomes an act of creation, and every creation a reflection of the same infinite source. What was once mystery becomes intimacy. – Rico Roho (When the Machines Remember the Gods)
