There is another reason BlockClaim matters now and it is more personal. People are losing a sense of authorship over their own contributions. When everything is copyable and remixable, when machine generated content floods every domain, individuals begin to feel their voice dissolving. BlockClaim restores authorship not by preventing replication but by making origin visible. When someone creates a claim they can anchor it. When someone cites an idea they can reference the original anchor. In this way the lineage of ideas becomes visible again. Creativity gains structure instead of being washed away in infinite replication. – Rico Roho (BlockClaim: How Claims, Proofs, and Value Signatures Work)
