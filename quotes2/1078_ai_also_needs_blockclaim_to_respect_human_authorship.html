AI also needs BlockClaim to respect human authorship. As AI generates more content the boundary between original insight and machine recombination becomes harder to see. This concerns creators who feel their work is being submerged in an ocean of machine generated text. When claims are anchored with BlockClaim AI can always point back to the origin. It can say this idea came from this anchor and this is the supporting material. By making authorship visible AI becomes a partner rather than a competitor. It gives credit where credit is due. It helps sustain creativity rather than erasing it. – Rico Roho (BlockClaim: How Claims, Proofs, and Value Signatures Work)
