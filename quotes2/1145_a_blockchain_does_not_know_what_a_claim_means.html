A blockchain does not know what a claim means.

A blockchain does not know what a claim means. It does not know whether a claim is true, ethical, harmful, or valuable. It cannot tell whether a claim should be acted upon or ignored. It cannot tell who should be held responsible. Any system that pretends otherwise quietly converts infrastructure into authority. - Rico Roho (Provenance Scroll 06 - What Blockchain Is Not Doing Here)
