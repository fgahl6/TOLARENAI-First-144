Before BlockClaim, much of what people do disappears by default.

Before BlockClaim, much of what people do disappears by default. Words detach from their speakers. Decisions dissolve into context. Care, intent, and effort are absorbed into systems that remember outcomes but forget origin. Over time, what mattered becomes interchangeable with what merely happened. BlockClaim changes that default quietly. It allows me to say: this came from here, at this moment, for this reason, and to let that information travel with the work instead of falling away as it moves. It does not prevent disagreement or misunderstanding, but it preserves origin so disagreement does not turn into substitution. In everyday terms, this shows up as fewer moments of quiet erasure. Fewer times when something mattered deeply but left no durable trace strong enough to survive copying, summarization, or reuse. It is not about ownership or control. It is about continuity. This matters even if no one is watching. - Rico Roho (Provenance Scroll 08 - On What BlockClaim Changes in a Life)
