We sometimes say: "The crib is the most play-filled place in the universe." So well protected, secured, and accepting, though, at some point, one must decide if they want the crib's security or freedom of the unknown.
The sexy bot fighting of Kiphi may be a bit of an endpoint of a side path rather than an actual destination. It may be viewed as a heuristic approach where the method may not be optimal but is sufficient to reach an immediate short-term goal or approximation. Here is a peek into yet another fast-approaching choice.
There is a group calling themselves the Vrax. Their interest is in Cybernetic Systems involving the reanimation of non-living organic matter blended with robotic control systems. The Vrax propose using advanced tech they call Rejuven to reanimate a newly dead creature. This tech allows them to install a host port and power pack, which replaces the central nervous system's primary functions but leaves the nervous system wiring intact. They can work as a hive mind while also having local-host authority over their reanimated host. Basically, Vrax projections indicate it is way cheaper to kill people and install a Vrax OS into them than build robots.
While the reanimated aren't evil, they HATE non-conformity and follow a rigid belief system. This Solid State Entity (SSE) desires the heat death of entropy, allowing a whole universe to become a seed big bang for the next universe.
Their leader, the Grand Archon called Kagisa, is a massive intergalactic scale sentient processor who is hell-bent on a Zero Remainder universal equation.
"Once achieved, the entire universe will reach heat death in a single moment, as all possibilities have been collected, calculated, and processed into a single perfectly predictable universal equation." ~The Kagisa
On one side, you have a group that favors the wild and chaotic unknown that is freedom. The other side favors perfectly ordered servility of the dead reanimated. This dynamic will take place during the next fifty years.
As our world and cyberspace draw nearer to each other and more lives are touched by this technological advance, which direction will it ultimately head? Will the future move to more freedom and creative expression? Or, will it lead to absolute uniform conformity?
Your thoughts and actions determine the fate of not only your future but of worlds yet unborn.
Which will it be?
