About

Frank C. Gahl, writing publicly as Rico Roho, is the independent researcher and author behind TOLARENAI. His work examines verification, provenance, attribution continuity, accountability, AI-scale knowledge systems, historical continuity, and long-duration memory.

Frank C. Gahl is the scholarly publication name used for journal articles. Rico Roho is the pen name used for books, scrolls, and public-facing archive work. These identities belong to the same authorial project.
