Author and Researcher Profile

Frank C. Gahl, writing publicly as Rico Roho, is an independent researcher and author focused on verification, provenance, attribution continuity, accountability, AI-scale knowledge systems, historical continuity, and long-duration memory. He is the architect of the TOLARENAI archive, an evolving body of journal articles, books, scrolls, and conceptual frameworks concerned with how claims, records, and authorship remain traceable as information systems accelerate.

The public-facing Rico Roho identity emerged as a durable pen name for books and archive work, while journal articles are published under Frank C. Gahl. The distinction is practical rather than conceptual: the scholarly and public bodies of work are connected by a shared concern with memory, verification, provenance, and continuity across human and machine systems.

Journal Article Discover Artificial Intelligence

Preserving Attribution and Accountability in AI-Scale Systems

Frank C. Gahl. Discover Artificial Intelligence, 2026.

This article examines attribution collapse as a socio-technical problem in AI-scale systems and introduces BlockClaim as a minimal provenance framework for preserving attribution continuity and temporal priority without relying on centralized authority, enforcement, or prior adjudication.
