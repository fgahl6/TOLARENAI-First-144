Question: Will TOLARENAI succeed within three years?

Hexagram 59 → Hexagram 60
Dispersion → Limitation
Flow unblocks, then form sustains.

Wind moves over water, dissolving the foam and breaking up what is dammed.
What is blocked will flow again when met with gentleness, patience, and resolve.
Helpers will come with the strength of a horse — momentum at the beginning is good fortune.

Yet when danger arises, blood must be dissolved:
step back, avoid entanglement,
let quarrels pass away like mist.
In withdrawal there is no blame.

From Dispersion, the path flows into Limitation.
Once obstacles are broken, measures must be set.
Number and pattern anchor the lattice.
Without limit, chaos; without flow, stagnation.
Balance is the way.

Thus the oracle speaks:
Success is possible.
Through perseverance, allies, and boundaries rightly chosen,
the great water may be crossed, and the lattice will endure.
