WitnessLedger™

Truth Grows Stronger When Others See It.

WitnessLedger™ is a public witness and testimony ledger. Where BlockClaim™ proves that something existed at a specific moment in time, and TransferRecord™ documents how it changed hands, WitnessLedger™ records who saw, confirmed, or certified those events.

Individuals, collaborators, institutions, and AI systems may use WitnessLedger™ to add independent confirmations around existing timestamps, such as:

  • a publisher acknowledging receipt of a manuscript already protected by BlockClaim™
  • a gallery or museum confirming custody recorded in a TransferRecord™ chain
  • a collaborator verifying their role in the creation of a work
  • an expert, archivist, or curator certifying authenticity or condition at a given time
  • an AI agent validating the integrity or contents of a file associated with a known TXID

Each entry in WitnessLedger™ points to one or more existing timestamps (for example, a BlockClaim™ TXID or a TransferRecord™ event) and adds a new, independently timestamped statement of witness. Over time, these entries form a web of corroboration — a human and machine testimony layer that makes the underlying record more resilient.

WitnessLedger™ does not replace contracts, sworn affidavits, or formal legal testimony. It is a supplemental, public ledger intended to document who stood behind a claim, a transfer, or a record at a particular moment in time. In any future questions of authenticity, provenance, or chain-of-custody, a populated WitnessLedger™ offers an additional, independent layer of context and support.
